# Consumable automations

Open **Automations** in the native game to change three saved site policies. All three start **On** in a new or older yard. The switches affect future actions; turning one off does not cancel a refueling job, supplier order, or collection that has already begun.

**Request vehicle refueling when low** queues an ordinary refueling job for equipment when its tank reaches the chosen percentage (25% initially). It waits for current work and manual control to end at a safe point. A shunter records a pending fuel request, then starts its existing driver-and-can service when parked, uncoupled, staffed by a qualified on-duty driver, and within 8 m of an accessible barrel. The pending reason appears in Automations if those conditions are missing. Fuel moves from a physical barrel to cans and tanks; nothing is topped up instantly.

**Order diesel for the site reserve** watches usable liters in stored barrels plus barrels still inbound. Fuel already in vehicle tanks is shown separately because it cannot refill another vehicle. Once any owned equipment or shunter first reaches the low-fuel threshold, the policy orders road-delivered 200 L drums when stored plus incoming fuel falls below the selected reserve (200 L initially). It orders enough whole drums to reach at least the selected target (400 L initially). An outstanding order counts toward the target, preventing repeated orders while the truck is coming. The first automatic order therefore waits for a real vehicle fuel need; you may still buy drums manually at any time. Delivery, unloading, storage, purchase cost, and later refueling remain physical.

**Collect empty diesel barrels** waits until that first fuel need, then requests the existing paid disposal service for unreserved empty drums. A collection truck comes to site and the normal crew and equipment must load it. Active refueling, storage movement, and another diesel collection retain their reservations; a second collection is not requested while the first remains active. Fees appear in Costs. Empty drums do not vanish when the switch is enabled.

The thresholds accept a whole refueling percentage from 1–90%, a reserve from 0–20,000 L, and a target from 200–20,000 L greater than the reserve. Change the numbers together with **Apply thresholds**. The Automations page shows current stored, incoming and tank fuel, low vehicles, pending shunter service, and empty barrel count. Every action is recorded in the normal orders, jobs, collection, cost, and event registers.
